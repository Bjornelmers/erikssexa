Add your custom audio files here!
If you place these exact filenames in this folder, the app will use them instead of the synthesized retro sound generator:

1. ambient_theme.mp3 - This will loop in the background as the main theme.
2. level_up.mp3 - This will play each time you increase the level.
3. victory_horn.mp3 - This will play when you reach level 1337.

Enjoy the stag party!
